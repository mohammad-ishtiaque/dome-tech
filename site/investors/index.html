01Investment thesis

Why Digital Dome, why Somalia, why now.

Four reasons we believe a focused local technology company can build durable value in this market.

A young market coming online

The median age is 15.6 (UN WPP 2024), and only 27.6% of people are online (DataReportal 2026). Most digital adoption is still ahead.

The payment rails already exist

About 73% of adults use mobile money (World Bank). Our products can charge from day one using methods people already trust.

Underserved by global platforms

Global products are not built for Somali language, geography, culture or payments. That leaves room for local platforms to lead.

Portfolio economics

Shared technology, brand and distribution lower the cost of each product. SomSoft enterprise revenue helps fund platform growth and reduces risk.

07Partnership strategy

Built to partner with telecom and mobile-money leaders.

Telecom operators and mobile-money providers are natural partners. They bring reach and payment infrastructure, and we bring products that create new digital transactions and engagement on their networks.

Discuss a partnership
  • Integration. Mobile-money payments embedded across products.
  • Distribution. Bundles, SMS and in-app promotion to reach subscribers at scale.
  • Commercial. Revenue-sharing and co-marketing on new digital services.
  • Investment. Strategic corporate investment aligned with digital-services growth.
  • Enterprise. SomSoft as a delivery partner for digital-transformation projects.
