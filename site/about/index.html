About Digital Dome

A technology company for the Somali digital economy.

Digital Dome is building technology platforms and software products designed around the realities, behavior and opportunities of the Somali digital economy.

01Who we are

One company, a portfolio of products.

Digital Dome is a technology company building a portfolio of digital products for Somalia and the wider Somali market. We are the parent company behind SomSpot, Shifaa, Fagaaro and SomSoft.

Who Digital Dome is

A professionally managed technology company with one brand, one leadership team and shared infrastructure across its products.

Why the market matters

Somalia is young, mobile-first and already pays with mobile money, yet most local digital services have not been built.

What we have built

Four products covering business discovery, trusted services, interactive events and enterprise software.

How we create value

Diversified revenue across advertising, commissions, subscriptions, SaaS and enterprise contracts.
